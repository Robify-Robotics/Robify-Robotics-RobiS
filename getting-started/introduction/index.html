🤖 Introduction to RobiS and RobiX Robots

Welcome to the RobiS and RobiX robot documentation. These robots are designed for research, education, and autonomous mobility scenarios with different configurations and onboard capabilities.

📋 RobiS Robot Specifications

Parameter Value
Dimensions (L × W × H) ≤ 450 × 380 × 390 mm
Chassis Weight ≤ 20 kg
Drive Type Four-wheel differential
Material Metal construction
Operating Temperature -10°C to 60°C
Obstacle Height (Step) 30 mm
Motor Model MD36 35W brushed DC motor
Encoder Resolution 500 PPR
Gear Ratio 1:51
Motor Current 0.3A (no load), 2.3A (rated), 7A (stall)
Battery Type Lithium Battery
Battery Capacity 24V 12AH
Battery Life ≤ 2 hours
Max Speed ≤ 1.5 m/s
Turning Radius In-place rotation (0°)
Communication Interface RS232 Serial
OS & Software Stack ROS / Autoware
Onboard Computer Jetson Orin Nano 4GB
